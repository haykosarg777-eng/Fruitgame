How to play & illustrated paytable

Pick a total bet (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 20, 50 or 100) and spin. Three or more of the same fruit on adjacent reels, starting at reel 1, win across 576 ways (3×4×4×4×3). Winning symbols may be on any row. The longest consecutive combination pays for each fruit. All upper positions are fully playable.

Above reels 2, 3 and 4 are the fourth selectable symbols on their respective reels. Each one has a fruit and a coefficient from 2× to 10×. A winning way may use a main symbol or the upper symbol on that reel. The upper coefficient applies only to ways that select the upper symbol. If a winning way uses two or three upper symbols, their coefficients multiply sequentially. An upper symbol cannot pay without a 3+ reel winning combination.

The breakdown groups winning ways by which upper positions they use. Each group pays total bet × group ways × fruit coefficient × that group’s selected upper multipliers. The reveal animates one featured group, then shows the combined spin total. The coefficients below are intentionally scaled for demonstration; the reel probabilities and payouts have not been certified or production-balanced.
